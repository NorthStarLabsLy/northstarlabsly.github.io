Private use and limitations

Terms

RED CENT Backup is a private utility operated by its owner for RED CENT backup and recovery. This information site does not offer an account, subscription or public backup service.

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Scope of use

The utility is intended for the owner's authorised Google Drive backup destination. Visiting these pages does not grant access to that destination, the RED CENT application or the utility's configuration.

These pages describe only the private backup utility. Access to other RED CENT software or data is outside their scope.

Owner responsibilities

The owner controls authorisation, the backup account and destination, credentials, recovery secrets, retention decisions and any deployment. The owner must use accounts and data they are authorised to access and comply with the relevant service providers' terms.

Credentials, archive contents and recovery secrets should not be submitted to this website. It has no upload or support-submission facility.

Operational limitations

The backup workflow remains subject to service availability, storage limits, OAuth expiry or revocation, configuration errors and loss of recovery secrets. These pages make no availability, uninterrupted-operation or successful-recovery commitment.

Local configuration checks and a read-only listing do not demonstrate an uploaded, verified and independently restored production archive. A recovery procedure needs its own qualification before the owner relies on it.

The prepared trial's backup-copy policy is described in the Privacy Policy. It is not a promise of indefinite retention or automatic erasure after a fixed period.

Utility status

These pages describe the private utility's intended scope. It does not announce a public RED CENT product launch or a deployed backup service.
