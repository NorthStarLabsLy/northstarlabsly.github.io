RED CENT Backup

An owner-operated utility for keeping encrypted copies of RED CENT backup archives in the owner's Google Drive.

A limited purpose

The utility uses rclone to transfer and verify encrypted backup archives in a designated Google Drive destination. Its purpose is private backup and recovery for the owner.

This is an information site for that backup utility. It is separate from RED CENT's application and is not a public sign-up, file-sharing or backup service.

Limited Google Drive access

The configured OAuth permission is drive.file. It permits access to specific files used with the app, rather than requesting access to all files in Google Drive. The utility is configured to use a dedicated backup destination.

The scope permits creating, reading, updating and deleting app-accessible files. The prepared backup workflow does not automatically delete remote snapshots.

Read the details

The Privacy Policy describes Google Drive access, encryption, retention and service-provider handling. The Terms explain the utility's private use and operational limitations.

This website does not provide Google sign-in, accept backup uploads or request credentials.

Utility status

The utility is prepared for private, owner-operated backups. It is not presented as a deployed or operationally qualified backup service.
